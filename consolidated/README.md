# Paper II — The Evaporation Invariant and its Generalisations

*Bharat Sharma — PhenexAI Research, Vancouver, BC, Canada*

The total information-geometry displacement over complete evaporation equals the outer-horizon radius:

$$R_{\text{total}} = \int_0^{M_0}\frac{dr_h}{dM}\,dM = r_h(M_0),$$

derived directly from the first law, covering the Schwarzschild, Kerr, Reissner–Nordström, Kerr–Newman, higher-dimensional, and AdS cases in one place. The factors f(χ), g(q), h(χ,q) are ratios of **Hawking temperatures**, not of integrated displacements. The result is an exact identity — a re-expression of the first law of black-hole mechanics.
